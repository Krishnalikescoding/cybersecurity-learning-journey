# Course 2 - Module 4: Network Attacks & Their Impact

## Why Network Security Matters

Networks are constantly targeted by **malicious actors** attempting to:

- Steal sensitive information
- Disrupt business operations
- Install malware
- Gain unauthorized access
- Damage an organization's reputation

Even a single successful attack can have severe financial, operational, and legal consequences.

---

# Common Network Attacks

## 1. Network Interception Attack

A **network interception attack** occurs when an attacker intercepts network traffic to steal, monitor, or modify data while it is being transmitted.

### Goals

- Steal confidential information
- Modify transmitted data
- Interrupt communications
- Insert malicious code

---

## Packet Sniffing

**Packet sniffing** is the process of capturing and inspecting data packets traveling across a network.

### Malicious Uses

- Stealing usernames and passwords
- Capturing credit card information
- Reading sensitive communications
- Monitoring user activity

### Example

An attacker intercepts an online banking transaction and changes the destination account number before the transaction reaches the bank.

---

# Other Network Interception Attacks

Examples include:

- **On-Path (Man-in-the-Middle) Attacks** – The attacker secretly intercepts communication between two parties.
- **Replay Attacks** – The attacker captures valid data and retransmits it later to gain unauthorized access.

---

# Backdoor Attack

A **backdoor attack** exploits hidden or intentionally created methods of bypassing normal authentication and security controls.

Backdoors may be:

- Created by developers for maintenance or troubleshooting
- Installed by attackers after compromising a system

### Why Attackers Use Backdoors

- Maintain persistent access
- Avoid detection
- Return to compromised systems later
- Install additional malware

---

# Denial-of-Service (DoS) Attack

A **Denial-of-Service (DoS)** attack floods a network or server with excessive traffic, making it unavailable to legitimate users.

### Effects

- Slow network performance
- Service outages
- Interrupted business operations
- Loss of availability

---

# Attack Summary

| Attack | Purpose | Impact |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Network Interception** | Capture or modify data in transit | Data theft, data manipulation |
| **Packet Sniffing** | Inspect network traffic | Credential and sensitive data theft |
| **Backdoor Attack** | Bypass authentication | Persistent unauthorized access |
| **DoS Attack** | Overwhelm systems with traffic | Service disruption and downtime |

# Impact on Organizations

## Financial Impact

- Lost revenue during outages
- Cost of incident response
- Infrastructure repair
- Legal penalties and lawsuits
- Ransomware payments
- Customer compensation

---

## Reputational Impact

- Loss of customer trust

## Public Safety Impact

Cyberattacks against critical infrastructure can affect:

- Power grids
- Water supply systems
- Government services
- Military communications
- Emergency response systems

These attacks may cause real-world harm to the public.

---

# Real-World Example

### Home Depot Data Breach (2014)

Attackers infected Home Depot's systems with malware and stole the payment card information of over **56 million customers**, demonstrating how devastating a successful network attack can be.

# Importance for Security Analysts

Security analysts are responsible for:

- Monitoring network activity
- Detecting suspicious behavior
- Identifying vulnerabilities
- Responding to incidents quickly
- Preventing unauthorized access
- Protecting organizational assets

---

# Key Takeaways

- **Network interception attacks** capture or alter data while it is being transmitted across a network.
- **Packet sniffing** is used to inspect network traffic and can be abused to steal sensitive information.
- **Backdoor attacks** bypass normal authentication, allowing attackers to maintain persistent access to compromised systems.
- A **Denial-of-Service (DoS)** attack overwhelms a network or server with traffic, making services unavailable to legitimate users.
- Cyberattacks can cause significant **financial losses**, **damage an organization's reputation**, and even threaten **public safety**, especially when critical infrastructure is targeted.
- Security analysts play a vital role in detecting threats, mitigating attacks, and protecting organizational networks from evolving cyber risks.
