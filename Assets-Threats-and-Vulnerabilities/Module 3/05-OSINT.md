# Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)

## Overview

Cyber attacks can sometimes be prevented with the right information, starting with knowing where systems are vulnerable. Previously covered methods of finding weaknesses include the **CVE list** and **scanning tools**. This reading introduces another method: **open-source intelligence (OSINT)**.

**OSINT:** The collection and analysis of information from publicly available sources to generate usable intelligence.

* Commonly used to support cybersecurity activities, such as identifying potential threats and vulnerabilities.

## Information vs. Intelligence

These terms are often used interchangeably, but they differ in focus and objective.

**Information:** The collection of raw data or facts about a specific subject.

**Intelligence:** The analysis of information to produce knowledge or insights that support decision-making.

* Intelligence is **derived from** information through analysis, interpretation, and integration.
* Both gathering information and developing intelligence are important aspects of cybersecurity.

### Example

* **Information:** A new update is released for the operating system (OS) installed on an organization's workstations.
* **Analysis:** Research across multiple cybersecurity news resources reveals new threats linked to this update.
* **Intelligence:** This analysis guides the organization's decision about whether and when to install the OS update on employee workstations.

## Information vs. Intelligence: Quick Comparison

| Information | Intelligence |
|---|---|
| Raw data or facts | Insights derived from analyzing information |
| Collected from a source | Produced through analysis, interpretation, and integration |
| Answers "what happened?" | Supports "what should we do?" |

## How Intelligence Improves Decision-Making

Businesses often use information to gain insight into customer behavior and improve decision-making. In security, open-source information is used similarly — to gain insight into **threats and vulnerabilities** that could pose risks to an organization.

OSINT plays a significant role in **Information Security (InfoSec)**.

**InfoSec:** The practice of keeping data in all states away from unauthorized users.

### Example

A company's InfoSec team is responsible for protecting the network from potential threats. They might:

1. Use OSINT to monitor online forums and hacker communities for discussions about emerging vulnerabilities.
2. Discover a forum post about a newly found weakness in software the company uses.
3. Quickly assess the risk.
4. Prioritize patching efforts.
5. Implement necessary safeguards to prevent an attack.

### Ways OSINT Can Be Used to Generate Intelligence

* Provide insights into cyber attacks.
* Detect potential data exposures.
* Evaluate existing defenses.
* Helps to identify unknown vulnerabilities.

Collecting intelligence is sometimes part of the **vulnerability management process** — security teams may use OSINT to build profiles of potential targets and make data-driven decisions to improve defenses.

## OSINT Tools

There is an enormous amount of open-source information available online, making it challenging to find relevant intelligence. Information can be gathered from many sources, including search engines, social media, discussion boards, and blogs.

### Common OSINT Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **[VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/home/upload)** | Analyzes suspicious files, domains, URLs, and IP addresses for malicious content |
| **[MITRE ATT&CK®](https://attack.mitre.org/)** | A knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques based on real-world observations |
| **[OSINT Framework](https://osintframework.com/)** | A web-based interface for finding OSINT tools for almost any source or platform |
| **[Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/)** | A tool that can be used to search for breached email accounts. |

Many other OSINT tools exist for finding specific types of information. It is the security professional's responsibility to thoroughly research any information relevant to the problem being solved.

## Key Takeaways

* Gathering **information** and developing **intelligence** are both important aspects of cybersecurity.
* OSINT supports **evidence-based decisions** that can help prevent attacks.
* Because so much information is available, security professionals need strong information-searching skills.
* Familiarity with popular OSINT tools and resources makes the intelligence-gathering process more effective.
