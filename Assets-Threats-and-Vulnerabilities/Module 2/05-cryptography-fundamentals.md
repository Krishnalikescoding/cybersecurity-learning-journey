# Personally Identifiable Information and Cryptography

## Overview

The internet is an open, public system where large amounts of data are constantly exchanged. However, some information needs to remain private.

One important type of private information is **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**.

**Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** Any information that can be used to infer an individual's identity.

Examples of PII include:

- Name
- Medical information
- Financial information
- Photos
- Emails
- Fingerprints

Protecting the privacy of PII online requires appropriate security controls.

## Cryptography

**Cryptography:** The process of transforming information into a form that unintended readers cannot understand.

Cryptography uses two main processes:

1. **Encryption:** Transforms readable information into an unreadable form.
2. **Decryption:** Transforms the unreadable information back into a readable form.

### Plaintext and Ciphertext

**Plaintext:** Data in its original, readable form.

**Ciphertext:** Data that has been transformed into an unreadable form through encryption.

Plaintext → Encryption → Ciphertext → Decryption → Plaintext
