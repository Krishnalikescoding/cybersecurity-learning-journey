# Course 8 – Terms and Definitions

## A

**AI Bias:** Bias in AI data or systems that can produce inaccurate, unfair, or unreliable results.

**Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Computer programs designed to perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, and understanding language.

## B

**Business Continuity Plan (BCP):** A document outlining procedures to maintain business operations during and after a major disruption.

## C

**Confidential Data:** Information with restricted access, available only to authorized individuals.

**Context (Prompting):** Background information provided to an AI tool to help it understand the task.

## D

**Data Controller:** A person or organization that determines why and how personal data is processed.

**Data Processor:** A person or organization that processes data on behalf of a data controller.

**Data Protection Officer (DPO):** An individual responsible for monitoring compliance with data protection requirements.

**Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP):** A plan outlining procedures to minimize the impact of incidents and restore affected systems and services.

## E

**Escalation Policy:** Procedures defining who should be notified about a security incident and how it should be handled.

**Evaluate (Prompting):** Reviewing AI-generated output to determine whether it meets the required expectations.

## G

**Generative AI (Gen AI):** A type of AI that creates new content, such as text, images, code, or other media, in response to prompts.

## H

**Human-in-the-Loop Approach:** A method of using AI with human supervision, involvement, and validation to improve reliability and responsible decision-making.

## I

**Improper Usage:** A security incident involving an employee violating an organization's acceptable use policies.

**Incident Escalation:** The process of identifying, triaging, and transferring a potential security incident to an appropriate or more experienced team member.

**Iterate (Prompting):** Repeatedly refining AI prompts and outputs to achieve better results.

## M

**Malware Infection:** A security incident in which malicious software infiltrates an organization's computers or network.

## O

**OWASP Top 10:** A globally recognized awareness document identifying the 10 most critical security risks to web applications.

## P

**Private Data:** Information that should not be accessible to the public.

**Prompt:** Input provided to an AI model to generate a specific response or content.

**Public Data:** Information accessible to the public that poses minimal risk if viewed or shared.

## R

**References (Prompting):** Examples or specific information provided to an AI tool to guide its output.

## S

**Security Mindset:** The ability to evaluate risks and continuously identify potential or actual security breaches involving systems, applications, or data.

**Sensitive Data:** Information requiring strong protection, including PII, SPII, and PHI.

**Stakeholder:** An individual or group interested in an organization's decisions or activities.

**STAR Method:** An interview storytelling framework used to explain experiences through Situation, Task, Action, and Result.

## T

**Task (Prompting):** The specific instruction or goal given to an AI model, often including the desired format or role.

**TCREI Framework:** A framework for writing effective AI prompts using Task, Context, References, Evaluate, and Iterate.

## U

**Unauthorized Access:** An incident in which someone gains physical or digital access to a system, application, or data without permission.

## V

**Visual Dashboard:** A tool that displays different types of data in one place using charts, graphs, or tables.
