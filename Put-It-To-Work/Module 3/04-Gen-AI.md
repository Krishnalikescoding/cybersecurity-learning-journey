# AI in Cybersecurity – Key Takeaways

## 1. TCREI Prompting Framework

The **TCREI framework** helps create effective prompts for generative AI tools.

| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| T – Task | Clearly explain what you want AI to do. |
| C – Context | Provide relevant background information. |
| R – References | Provide examples, sources, or reference material. |
| E – Evaluate | Review the AI-generated output for accuracy and usefulness. |
| I – Iterate | Refine the prompt to improve the results. |

**Memory Trick:** Thoughtfully Create Really Excellent Inputs.

## 2. Growing Role of AI in Cybersecurity

AI is becoming increasingly important in cybersecurity.

Security professionals can use AI to:
- Identify potential security risks.
- Automate security responses.
- Analyze threats and prioritize incidents.
- Improve productivity and decision-making.
- Secure AI systems used by their organizations.

## 3. Generative AI in Cybersecurity

**Generative AI (Gen AI)** is a type of AI capable of creating new content based on user prompts.

### Common Applications

1. **Content Creation:** Generate cybersecurity best practices and security awareness guidelines.
2. **Data Analysis:** Analyze and summarize large security reports.
3. **Threat Understanding:** Explain common cybersecurity threats and attack techniques.
4. **Phishing Detection:** Analyze suspicious emails for signs of phishing.
5. **Automation:** Simplify repetitive daily tasks.

## 4. Responsible Use of Generative AI

AI tools have limitations and must be used responsibly.

### Important Guidelines

1. **Verify outputs:** Check AI-generated information for accuracy and usefulness.
2. **Disclose AI usage:** Be transparent about using AI when required.
3. **Protect privacy:** Avoid entering sensitive or confidential organizational information.
4. **Human-in-the-loop:** Always involve human judgment in reviewing AI outputs and making important decisions.
5. **Follow company policies:** Use AI according to your organization's guidelines.

**Human-in-the-loop:** An approach in which humans supervise, review, and validate AI-generated results instead of blindly relying on AI.

## 7. Key Takeaways

- AI knowledge is becoming an important skill for cybersecurity professionals.
- AI helps identify risks, prioritize threats, automate tasks, and investigate alerts.
- Generative AI can create content, analyze reports, explain threats, and assist with phishing detection.
- AI-generated results must always be reviewed by humans.
- Sensitive organizational information should never be entered into unapproved AI tools.
- Cybersecurity professionals may also be responsible for securing AI systems.
