# Business Continuity Planning

- **Business Continuity Plan (BCP):** A document outlining procedures to maintain business operations during and after a major disruption.
- **Purpose:** Minimize interruptions and restore critical business functions quickly.
- **Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP):** Focuses on recovering IT systems after a disaster, such as hardware failure or a flood.
- **BCP vs DRP:** BCP maintains business operations, while DRP focuses on restoring information systems.

## Impact of Ransomware on Business Continuity

- Ransomware can encrypt data and make critical systems inaccessible.
- In healthcare, it can prevent access to medical records and disrupt essential services.
- Attacks on critical infrastructure can threaten public safety, economic security, and national security.
- BCPs help minimize disruptions and maintain essential services.

## Recovery Strategies

Organizations need recovery strategies to restore systems and return to normal operations after an incident.

### Site Resilience

**Resilience:** The ability to prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruptions while maintaining services.

Types of recovery sites:

1. **Hot Site:** A fully operational duplicate of the primary site. Can be activated immediately.
2. **Warm Site:** A configured and updated backup site that requires some additional work before becoming operational.
3. **Cold Site:** A backup facility with basic infrastructure that requires significant setup before it can operate.

## Key Takeaways

- BCPs help organizations continue essential operations during and after disruptions.
- DRPs focus on recovering IT systems and infrastructure.
- Site resilience improves service availability during failures.
- Hot sites offer the fastest recovery, followed by warm sites and then cold sites.
